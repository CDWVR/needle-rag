Ottermere Labs Employee Handbook
Edition 2026.1 - for employees in the United Kingdom and the European Union

Welcome
This handbook explains how we work: time off, where and when we work, expenses, equipment, and security. It does not replace your employment contract. Where the two differ, your contract wins.

Questions about this handbook go to the People team through the HR portal.
\f
Time off

Paid time off accrues at 1.75 days per full month of employment, which is 21 days a year, on top of public holidays in your country of employment.

You can carry over at most 10 unused days into the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire if they are not used by 31 March.

Sick leave is separate from paid time off. Tell your manager on the first morning you are unwell; a doctor's note is needed for absences longer than 5 working days.

Parental leave: the primary caregiver receives 20 weeks at full pay and the secondary caregiver receives 8 weeks at full pay, taken within the first year.
\f
Where and when we work

Most roles are hybrid. Hybrid employees are expected in the office at least two days a week, and every team agrees one fixed anchor day per week.

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone. Outside core hours you choose when to work, as long as your team can reach you for planned meetings.

Field service engineers follow the shift rota for their region instead of core hours. On-call engineers receive a stipend of 350 EUR for each full week on call, plus time off in lieu for any call-out that lasts longer than 2 hours.
\f
Expenses and equipment

Day-to-day expenses such as meals with customers are capped at 60 EUR per person per day. Keep a receipt for anything above 25 EUR. Travel expenses follow the current travel policy, not this page.

New starters receive a laptop, a monitor, and a 400 EUR home-office allowance that can be claimed once in the first 90 days.

Laptops are replaced every 3 years. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours.
\f
Security

Every employee completes security awareness training within 14 days of their start date, and again every 12 months.

Multi-factor authentication is required on every company account. Passwords must not be reused across accounts and are stored in the company password manager.

Report anything suspicious, including unexpected emails asking for credentials, to the security team through the #security-report channel. Reporting quickly matters more than being sure.
