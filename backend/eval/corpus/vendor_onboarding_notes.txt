Vendor onboarding notes — lidar suppliers

Current supplier
Ottermere buys every lidar unit for the Tern-3 from a single supplier under a contract that runs until June 2027. Lead time for new units is currently 11 weeks. Each unit is burn-in tested for 48 hours at the Leeds depot before it is fitted to a cart.

Second-source qualification plan
Qualification of a second supplier has three gates: a bench test of 20 sample units, a 500-hour soak test on four carts at the Tilburg site, and a pricing review. A supplier must pass all three gates before any purchase order is raised. The target is to finish qualification by December 2026.
