# Travel Policy 2026

Ottermere Labs · Effective 1 January 2026 · Replaces the 2025 travel policy

## What changed from 2025
The economy-class threshold rises from 6 hours to 8 hours, the standard hotel cap rises to 240 USD, the per diem rises to 65 USD, and expense reports are now due within 21 days instead of 30.

## Booking
All flights, rail, and hotels are booked through the company travel desk at least 14 days before departure where possible. Bookings made fewer than 7 days before departure need written approval from a director.

## Flights
Economy class is required for flights shorter than 8 hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights of 8 hours or longer. Business class is reimbursed only for flights longer than 11 hours that are followed by a working day within 12 hours of landing, and needs approval from a vice president.

## Hotels
The nightly hotel cap is 240 USD in most cities and 320 USD in London, New York, Zurich, and Oslo, including taxes.

## Per diem
Meals on travel days are covered by a flat per diem of 65 USD per day. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Ground transport
Rail is preferred over flying for journeys under 4 hours. Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursed between the airport or station and the hotel or site. Car rentals need prior approval and must be the smallest class that fits the equipment being carried.

## Expense reports
Travel expense reports must be filed within 21 days of the trip's end date, with receipts for every item above 25 USD. Reports filed later than 60 days after the trip are not reimbursed.
