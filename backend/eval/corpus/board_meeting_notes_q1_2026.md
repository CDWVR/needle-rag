# Board meeting notes — Q1 2026

Ottermere Labs · Meeting held 26 February 2026 · Minutes approved 12 March 2026

## Attendees
Board: Halvard Ostby (chair), Mireille Okonkwo, Daniel Fairweather, Sun-hee Park. Management: Chief Executive Ana Lucía Ferreira, Chief Financial Officer Rohan Mehta, VP Engineering Katarzyna Lis.

## Financial update
The CFO reported fiscal 2025 revenue of 38.6 million USD, up 41 percent year over year, with gross margin of 52 percent. Cash on hand at year end was 27.1 million USD, which the CFO said gives 26 months of runway at the current burn.

## Decisions
1. The board approved opening a fourth European service hub in Rotterdam, targeted to open in Q3 2026, to support the Benelux and German customer base.
2. The board approved a research budget of 2.4 million EUR for the Tern-4 programme in 2026, focused on a 200 kg payload and a lift-deck variant.
3. Hiring for general and administrative roles is frozen until the end of Q2 2026. Engineering, field service, and sales hiring continue as planned.
4. The board asked management to bring a proposal for a cold-storage-native cart to the Q2 meeting.

## Risks discussed
- Supplier concentration: one vendor supplies all lidar units. Management will qualify a second lidar supplier by the end of 2026.
- Reliability: the board asked for an audit of every manually renewed credential in the Fleet Manager stack before the Q2 meeting.

## Next meeting
The Q2 board meeting is scheduled for 21 May 2026 in Porto.
