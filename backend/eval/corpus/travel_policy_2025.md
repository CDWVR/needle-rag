# Travel Policy 2025

Ottermere Labs · Effective 1 January 2025 · Superseded on 1 January 2026

## Booking
All flights, rail, and hotels are booked through the company travel desk at least 14 days before departure where possible. Bookings made fewer than 7 days before departure need written approval from a director.

## Flights
Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class is not reimbursed under this policy.

## Hotels
The nightly hotel cap is 210 USD in most cities and 290 USD in London, New York, Zurich, and Oslo, including taxes.

## Per diem
Meals on travel days are covered by a flat per diem of 55 USD per day. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Ground transport
Rail is preferred over flying for journeys under 4 hours. Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursed between the airport or station and the hotel or site.

## Expense reports
Travel expense reports must be filed within 30 days of the trip's end date, with receipts for every item above 25 USD.
