# Public demo documents

These files are loaded into the public demo (`NEEDLE_DEMO=1`) and used by the `demo` eval suite.
Files whose names start with `_` or `.` are skipped, so this notice is never indexed.

They are included for educational purposes only, to show how Needle answers questions about
lecture material:

- `Autoencoders.pdf` and `Attention Mechanism.pdf.txt` are teaching slides. As the slides
  themselves state, their content is not original and was prepared from various sources for
  teaching. The sources are credited in the slides.
- `rnn_basics.txt` and `information_retrieval_basics.txt` are short notes written for this repo.

If you hold rights to any of this material and want it removed, open an issue and it will be taken down.
